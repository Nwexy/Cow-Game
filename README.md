## 🎮 How to Play

- **Click:** Left-click the cow or press **Spacebar**.
- **Level Up:** Every **50 points** increases your level and multiplies click power by **1.25x**.
- **Shop:** Buy farm upgrades (Calf, Farmer, Barn, Windmill) for passive income.
- **Autosave:** Your progress saves automatically.
